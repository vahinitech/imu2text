Vahini playground

A pen with motion sensors writes one letter. Follow it through the pipeline: the raw signal, a filter, the model, and what the model thinks it saw. Every number on this page comes from a run in the imu2text repository.

1 Pick a letter

Real test letters from writers the model never saw during training.

2 The signal, and a filter

13 channels at 100 Hz: two accelerometers, a gyroscope, a magnetometer and the pen-tip force.

Raw values

Drag the slider or move over a plot. Values are raw sensor counts; g and °/s use the conversions documented in imu2text/filters.py (the gyroscope one is inferred, not from a datasheet).

3 The model's answer

Five copies of the same network, trained with different random seeds. Each gives a probability for all 52 letters. The ensemble averages them.

Data. The OnHW datasets are by Fraunhofer IIS and are for non-commercial use. They are not covered by this repository's Apache-2.0 license and are not redistributed here: the published page shows model outputs and a synthetic signal, and a local build adds the recordings you downloaded yourself. Cite: Ott, Wehbi, Hamann, Barth, Eskofier and Mutschler, "The OnHW Dataset: Online Handwriting Recognition from IMU-Enhanced Ballpoint Pens with Machine Learning", Proc. ACM IMWUT 4(3), 2020.

Code: Apache-2.0. Build the data with python -m scripts.build_playground; see playground/README.md.
