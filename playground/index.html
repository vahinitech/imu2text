2 The signal, and a filter

13 channels at 100 Hz: two accelerometers, a gyroscope, a magnetometer and the pen-tip force.

Raw values

Drag the slider or move over a plot. Values are raw sensor counts; g and °/s use the conversions documented in imu2text/filters.py (the gyroscope one is inferred, not from a datasheet).

What the sensors measure

The accelerometers, one at the front of the pen and one at the back, measure acceleration along three axes, including gravity: a pen at rest reads about 1 g, 16,384 counts, split across its axes by how it is tilted. The gyroscope measures how fast the pen rotates. The magnetometer measures the Earth's magnetic field, which changes with the pen's heading. The force sensor measures how hard the tip presses, and falls when the pen lifts. The pen records all 13 values 100 times a second.

None of them measures where the tip is. The model has to read the letter from how the pen moves, which is why size, and so upper versus lower case, is hard to see.
